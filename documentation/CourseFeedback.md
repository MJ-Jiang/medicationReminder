## What went well and helped you in your studying?

- Google Translator helped me translate Finnish documents into English.
- The teacher always responded quickly, and I really appreciate that.
- YouTube tutorial videos helped me a lot.

## What went wrong and hindered you in your studying?

- I remember that starting from the third exercise, things became much more difficult and I lost some confidence.
- There were interruptions throughout the process, which made me almost forget the code I had written after just two weeks.
- The submission process was unclear to me. At first, I only submitted the quiz to indicate that I had completed the five exercises.  
  Then I realized I should write the evaluation in the "Assignment submission" section.  
  However, it wasn’t until a month later that I found out I was actually supposed to submit it there —  
  this submission process is different from the usual one.

## How should the course be further developed?

- I hope the course materials can be translated into English so that international students can follow more easily.
- I think the five exercises are really interesting. It would be great if we could see other students' answers.
- Maybe we could consider a process that allows students to present their exercises or projects in short videos?
